RESHAPElab

Research in Software: Human Aspects, Practices and Education School of Informatics, Computing and Cyber Systems · Northern Arizona University

Software is built by people. We study the conditions they need.

We work on the human side of software engineering — how newcomers get in, why maintainers burn out, how governance holds a project together, and what AI changes about all three.
