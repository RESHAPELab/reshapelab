RESHAPE Lab hat trick at ICSE 2024!

3/1/24 · papers

RESHAPE Lab will be very well represented at ICSE 2024 in Lisbon! Members of our lab are co-authors of three papers in the Research Track that will be presented at the conference:

  • 'How Far Are We? The Triumphs and Trials of Generative AI in Learning Software Engineering' by Rudrajit Choudhuri, Dylan Liu, Igor Steinmacher, Marco Gerosa, Anita Sarma
  • 'How to Support ML End-User Programmers through a Conversational Agent' by Emily Judith Arteaga Garcia, João Felipe Pimentel, Zixuan Feng, Marco Gerosa, Igor Steinmacher, Anita Sarma
  • 'Unraveling the Drivers of Sense of Belonging in Software Delivery Teams: Insights from a Large-Scale Survey' by Bianca Trinkenreich, Marco Gerosa, Igor Steinmacher

Two of the papers are part of projects in collaboration with EPIC Lab from Oregon State University, and the last one is a paper with Bianca Trinkenreich in industry context.

We will be well represented in Lisbon! 'Nos vemos lá!'
