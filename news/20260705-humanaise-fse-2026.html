Two papers on generative AI at HumanAISE 2026, co-located with FSE

Jul 5, 2026 · papers

Lab members co-authored two papers at HumanAISE 2026, the 2nd Workshop on Human-Centered AI for Software Engineering, co-located with FSE 2026 in Montréal.

  • "Shh! Don't Tell Them I Use GenAI: Exploring How Participants Use Generative AI in Hackathons" by Pawan Acharya, Tayana Conte, Jacob Penney, Pedro Oliveira, Misan Paul Etchie, Jared Duval, Bruno Gadelha, Marco Gerosa and Igor Steinmacher (paper)
  • "The Fast and Spurious: Developer Productivity with GenAI" by Sadia Afroz, Zixuan Feng, Tyler Menezes, Katie Kimura, Bianca Trinkenreich, Igor Steinmacher and Anita Sarma (paper)
