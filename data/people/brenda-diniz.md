Brenda Diniz is a doctoral student at the Federal University of Mato Grosso (UFMT). Her current research focuses on environmental resources and environmental monitoring, with an emphasis on sustainable strategies for conservation and management of natural resources. In the academic field, Brenda also develops projects aimed at environmental education, with a focus on the development of scientific books aimed at children. Her experience also covers areas such as environmental projects, environmental certification, project management and environmental education.
