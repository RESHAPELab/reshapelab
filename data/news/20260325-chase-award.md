We are proud to share that our paper **"Governance in Practice: How Open Source
Projects Define and Document Roles"** has received an **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished
Paper Award** at CHASE 2026 🎉

Congratulations to [Pedro Oliveira](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pedro-oliveira-3698141a3/), the PhD student who led this study, and to the full team:

- [Tayana Conte](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tayana-conte-2b298a9/)
- [Marco Aurélio Gerosa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marco-aur%C3%A9lio-gerosa-3224621a/)
- [Igor Steinmacher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/igorsteinmacher/)

A collaborative work between [RESHAPE Lab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/reshape-lab-nau/) at NAU and [USES — Grupo de Pesquisa em Usabilidade e Engenharia de Software](https://www.linkedin.com/company/usesufam/).

This work investigates how open source projects formalize roles, responsibilities, and authority through governance documents. Our findings show that even when projects use similar role names, their responsibilities often differ significantly — revealing inconsistencies we call *role drift*. We also highlight how maintainers frequently accumulate technical, managerial, and community responsibilities, raising important implications for sustainability and burnout in OSS communities.

This recognition reflects the impact of studying not just code, but the human and organizational structures that sustain open source ecosystems.
