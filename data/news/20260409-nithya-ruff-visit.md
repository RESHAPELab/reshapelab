We had the pleasure of hosting [Nithya Ruff](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nithyaruff/) at Northern Arizona University.

Nithya delivered an outstanding talk as part of the Open Source course and AZ Tech Week, reflecting on the past, present, and future of open source software through the lens of her own career. It was a rare opportunity for students to connect the evolution of OSS with real-world leadership, decision-making, and impact.

Before the talk, she also took the time to engage with our students and learn more about the work we do at RESHAPE Lab — particularly around OSS governance, sustainability, AI, and education. Nithya's curiosity and generosity left a strong impression.

Many thanks to [Cheyenne Carpenter](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cheyenne-carpenter-625356a6/) and [Tina Zecher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tina-zecher/) from the NAU Office of Undergraduate Research and Creative Activity, and to the NAU Steve Sanghi College of Engineering, for helping make this visit possible.

These are the kinds of experiences that stay with students long after the class ends.
