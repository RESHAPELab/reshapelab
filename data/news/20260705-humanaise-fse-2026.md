Lab members co-authored two papers at [HumanAISE 2026](https://humanai4se.github.io/),
the 2nd Workshop on Human-Centered AI for Software Engineering, co-located with
FSE 2026 in Montréal.

- **"Shh! Don't Tell Them I Use GenAI: Exploring How Participants Use Generative AI in Hackathons"** by Pawan Acharya, Tayana Conte, Jacob Penney, Pedro Oliveira, Misan Paul Etchie, Jared Duval, Bruno Gadelha, Marco Gerosa and Igor Steinmacher ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3803437.3806711))
- **"The Fast and Spurious: Developer Productivity with GenAI"** by Sadia Afroz, Zixuan Feng, Tyler Menezes, Katie Kimura, Bianca Trinkenreich, Igor Steinmacher and Anita Sarma ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3803437.3806710))
