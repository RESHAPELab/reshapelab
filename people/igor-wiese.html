About

I completed a PhD in Computer Science at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of São Paulo in March 2016. I am a Full Professor at the Federal University of Technology - Paraná (UTFPR), Brazil. I was a Visiting Scholar at the University of California, Irvine, from July 2013 to January 2014 and a post-doctoral Scholar at Northern Arizona University (NAU) from July 2019 to February 2021. Most of my research uses AI and Data Science techniques to leverage software engineering tasks.
